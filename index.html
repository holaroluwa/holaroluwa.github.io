The build

A working outbound system in two weeks, for £750. It's made of four parts, built in this order.

  1. 01Buyer list

    I find your ideal buyers.

    Each one is researched with AI and checked by a human before they go on the list. You end up with people who fit what you sell.

    Researched with AI, checked by a human

  2. 02Sequences

    I write the email and LinkedIn sequences.

    Every touch is written for the buyers on your list, in plain words you'd be happy to send yourself.

    Email and LinkedIn

  3. 03Sending

    I set up the sending so your emails land in the inbox, not spam.

    That means authenticating your domains and warming them up slowly before any real volume goes out. Good words don't help if nobody sees them.

    Inbox, not spam

  4. 04Tracker

    I hand over a simple pipeline tracker you can run yourself.

    Replies, booked calls and proposals sit on one board. If you'd rather not run it yourself, I'll run it for you for £2,500 a month.

    Simple enough to run yourself
